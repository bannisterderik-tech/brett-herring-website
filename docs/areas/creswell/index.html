Creswell, Oregon

Creswell: small town on the interstate, ten minutes from eugene

Creswell is a small city on I-5 just south of the metro that has grown steadily because the math works: you get a real downtown, a school district of its own, and a genuinely short commute. It has an airport, a main street, and enough farm ground around it to feel like a town rather than a suburb.

The property picture

What you will actually find.

A mix of in-town homes on city services, newer subdivisions, and rural acreage in the surrounding farm country. Parts of the area have historically fallen within USDA eligible boundaries, which matters for buyers needing low down payment options. Check by address.

Living here

Small-town pace with metro access. The commute north is a straightforward interstate drive.

Schools

Creswell School District serves the community.

Talk Creswell property
Local knowledge

Things you learn by living here.

  • The town is named for John Creswell, who was the twenty-third Postmaster General of the United States. The first store opened in 1872 and the town grew up around it. It incorporated in 1909.
  • Creswell went from roughly 3,600 people in 2000 to about 5,600 in 2020. That is the commuter math working out in public.
