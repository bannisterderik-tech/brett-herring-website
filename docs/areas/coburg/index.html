Coburg, Oregon

Coburg: a historic district with an interstate exit

Coburg is small, old, and genuinely charming, a town of nineteenth-century buildings with a National Register historic district, sitting just off I-5 north of the metro. The town was originally called Diamond, after a pioneer. The name it kept came from a stallion, which had itself been named after a district in Bavaria.

The property picture

What you will actually find.

Genuine historic homes in the district, which come with both character and the maintenance realities of nineteenth-century construction. Newer homes and rural acreage in the surrounding area. Historic district status can affect exterior alterations, which is worth understanding before you buy with plans.

Living here

A walkable historic core, farm country around it, and the metro ten minutes down the interstate.

Schools

Eugene School District 4J serves Coburg.

Talk Coburg property
Local knowledge

Things you learn by living here.

  • The historic district was listed on the National Register in 1986, and the period of significance reaches back to 1875. Downtown holds one of the most intact nineteenth-century streetscapes in the county.
  • Buying in a historic district means design review can apply to exterior changes. Ask before you plan a remodel, not after.
  • Coburg is in the Eugene 4J school district, which surprises people who assume anything north of the metro belongs to a different district.
