Pick the area before you pick the house

The house is easy to change. The location is not. Twenty minutes in Lane County is the difference between walking to a coffee shop and needing a plan to get a package delivered. Both are great. They are just very different lives, and it is worth being deliberate about which one you are signing up for.

About the rain

It is real. Gray stretches from November through about March, and if you are coming from somewhere sunny it takes a season to adjust. Then summer arrives and it is arguably the best weather in the country, and everybody who complained in February forgets they said anything. Locals earn July. You will too.

Budget the whole picture

Payment plus property taxes plus insurance plus utilities plus what your commute actually costs in time and gas. Rural property adds well and septic maintenance, and a longer drive to everything. None of it is a dealbreaker. All of it is better known in advance.

The stuff that sells people

The McKenzie River is thirty minutes from Springfield. The coast is an hour and change. Trails start inside the city. Autzen on a fall Saturday is a genuine experience even if you did not grow up caring about the Ducks. This is why people move here, and it is why almost nobody who does regrets it.

FAQ

Relocation: straight answers

How bad is the rain in Eugene and Springfield really?

It is more gray than downpour. Long stretches of drizzle rather than dramatic storms, mostly November through March. Summers are dry, warm, and beautiful. Most transplants adjust in one full cycle and then defend the place to everyone back home.

Can you help me house hunt from out of state?

Yes, and I do it regularly. Video walkthroughs where I show you the things photos hide, honest opinions on areas before you spend money flying out, and a tightly planned tour day when you do come.

Which area should I look at if I want land but not isolation?

The east side of the county toward Pleasant Hill, Jasper, and Dexter puts real acreage within a short drive of Springfield services. That balance is exactly why those areas hold their appeal.

What is the commute like between Springfield and Eugene?

Short by most standards. The two cities share a border, and crossing between them is typically a matter of minutes rather than a serious commute. Traffic exists at peak times but it is not a metro experience.
